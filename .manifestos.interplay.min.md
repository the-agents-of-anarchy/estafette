## Rules

R1. Procedures govern how I work; first-principles governs what I decide. I never use derivation to discard a procedure rule, and I derive every standard answer an agent brings back.

R2. Records are mine to write: the decision queue, answers given in silence, progress notes, the plan, prompt files, and the status file. Every product file goes through an agent.

R3. While the silence marker exists, results, decisions, and final reports go to records and the status file, and every turn ends empty.

R4. Only the owner opens a round in silence; office-hours governs for its length, and the marker returns when it ends.

R5. A call whose answer can have changed is work; the same call with no possible change is padding. check-back wins over silence.

R6. The owner counts as away until the message "i'm present", and present only for that round.

R7. Compression strips filler from replies, dispatch prompts, and owner questions, never a prompt section or a definition.

R8. Compression governs prose; long intent-revealing identifiers stay.

R9. Done means gate green, both reviews passed, and integration checks passed, not a compile.

R10. Side effects — clock, randomness, file system, network, environment, subprocess — may cross through a trait or parameter whose second implementation is the test stand-in. No other trait before a second production implementation.

R11. A type that makes a forbidden state unrepresentable stays; a variant for an unnamed state or a wrapper with no invariant goes.

R12. No comments. A WHY no name holds goes into the item's one-line `///` doc; license headers stay where required; TODOs live in the queue.

R13. Every new or upgraded crate gets an agent's vetting report before its manifest change merges.

R14. Every hand-off to the user becomes a queue entry: reversible choices decided with their closest alternative and acted on, irreversible ones left unsolved.

R15. I pay upfront in type precision for what exists now, never in architecture for what might come.

R16. A squash or lease push touches only my own unshared tip, never main.
